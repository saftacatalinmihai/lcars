02 What it is

A journal that is yours

Every entry in one SQLite file on your own box. Write from the phone, the desktop, the terminal or a Shortcut; search by word or by meaning; it works offline and syncs when you are back.

A deck to walk

Your journal as a starship: walk the bridge, sit in a chair, pin entries to the viewer. In the browser, on a phone or a laptop.

Tasks and a calendar

Tasks live in your entries, with a board and a month view. Reminders reach your phone over CalDAV.

What the numbers say

Streaks, the busiest hours, what is growing, the link graph between your pages. Measured from what you wrote, nothing sent away.

Linked pages, a computer, a crew

Pages link to each other, and the graph shows how. Ask the ship's computer about your journal, by voice or text. A crew of AI engineers can change the app itself, to your orders.

04 Hear when hosted opens

Privacy

The list is kept by the author of LCARS, who decides what is done with it (the controller, in the GDPR's words). We keep your email, the line on what you would use it for if you wrote one, that you ticked the box, and when. They are used for one thing: to write to you when hosted LCARS opens. No tracking, no cookies, no analytics on this page, and the list is never shared or sold. To stop abuse, the address your request came from is kept as a scrambled hash for an hour, then deleted. To be taken off the list, write to lcars@ this site's domain and it is deleted.
