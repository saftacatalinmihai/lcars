## 5. Tasks and the calendar {#tasks}

A task is a line in any entry that starts with `TODO` or another task word (below); the task board (**Tasks**{ui}, or **t**) lists every open one in the journal, oldest first.

### Write a task

Start a line with one of these words:

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| `TODO`, `LATER` | to do |
| `DOING`, `NOW` | under way |
| `WAITING` | waiting on someone |
| `DONE` | finished |
| `CANCELED` | dropped |

For example, `- TODO call the garage` in today's log is a task. **Task**{ui} in New's menu (or "computer, new task") starts an entry with a `TODO` line ready.

### See and tick your tasks

1. Tap **Tasks**{ui} in the filters, or press **t**.
2. The board lists each open task line, with the entry it is in. **Filters**{ui} at the top chooses **Open**{ui}, **Done**{ui} or **All**{ui}, which kinds, and which dates. On a phone they fold to one line; tap it to open them.
3. Tap the circle beside a task to tick it **DONE**. The message at the bottom offers **Undo**{ui}.
4. Tap the task's text to open its entry.

![The task board on a phone: Filters set to Open, a count of task lines, and the tasks under DOING, each with its kind and age and a circle to tick.](img/tasks.webp "The task board. The colour beside each task is its entry's kind.")

### Give a task a due date and a reminder

Entries of the task kind (`task` unless you changed it in Config) are also reminders. They can be read by a phone's or a computer's reminders app over CalDAV, if your server is set up for it.

1. Open the task's entry. Tap **reminders**{ui}, beside when it was written.
2. Set the due date, the alerts and the repeat. Every other CalDAV field is there too.

Or say it: "computer, due friday at 5 pm", "computer, remind me when it is due".

### Open the calendar

Tap **Month**{ui} in the filters, or press **M**. Choose the scale at the top: **Year**, **Month**{ui}, **Week** or **Day** (keys **y**, **m**, **w**, **d**). The arrows (or **←** and **→**) move a period.

- A tap on a day shows its entries under the calendar. **Day** opens that day hour by hour; **In the list**{ui} filters the list to it.
- **Today**{ui}, **This week**{ui}, **This month**{ui} or **This year**{ui}, by the scale, comes back to now.

![The calendar's week on a phone: the week's dates with the arrows, Year Month Week Day, This week, the seven days with a dot on the day that has an entry, and that day's entries below.](img/calendar.webp "The week. A dot marks a day with entries.")

### Move an entry to another day or hour

At a desk, drag an entry in the calendar onto another day or hour. **That changes when it was written**, as editing **Written**{ui} in its Details would.

In the day view, drag across empty hours to make a new entry at that time. Drag a block's top or bottom edge to change when it starts or ends.

On a phone, open the entry and change **Written**{ui} in its Details.

### Plan an hour

Say "computer, plan an hour", or tap a free hour in the day view. Today's next free hour is scheduled with a name you type in place. The message offers **Undo**{ui}.

### Time something

Open the entry and say "computer, start the timer" (or open "the stopwatch"). It counts up from now; "stop the timer" ends it. The start and end are kept in the entry's **Scheduled**{ui}.
