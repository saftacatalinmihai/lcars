## Appendix B. Every spoken phrase {#phrases}

### How voice orders work {#voice}

Tap **Voice**{ui} in the header (or press **m**) to open the microphone. Then say "computer," and an order: "computer, new entry", "computer, search for garage". "Computer, help" shows this list in the console; "stand down" closes the microphone.

- **Dictation**{ui} types what you say into the field that has the cursor. While it is on, an order needs "computer" in front, so your words are not taken as orders.
- **With dictation paused** ("pause dictation"), the console answers orders without "computer".
- A phrase with `{…}` takes your words in its place: "search for {…}" is "search for the dentist".
- "Run {…}" runs anything in the command palette by its name, for what has no phrase of its own.

A few things cannot be done by voice because the browser allows them only from a tap: choosing a file, the camera, and the share sheet of your phone. The password of classified entries is typed, never said.

The table is the console's own list, made from the app at each build. Many phrases have other wordings too; "computer, help" shows them.
