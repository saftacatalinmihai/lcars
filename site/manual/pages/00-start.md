## 0. Start here: what is the console? {#start}

The console is the web app this manual covers: your journal, read and written in a browser, on a phone or at a desk.

### The three words to know

- **Entry.** An entry is one thing written down in the journal, on a day, with a kind. A note, a task, a night's sleep and a trip are all entries.
- **Kind.** A kind is the entry's type, named in your own words. A new journal starts with one kind, `personal_log`, shown as **Personal log**: every new entry gets it until you choose another. To add another, type a name nobody has used in the kind picker: its last row is **new kind**{ui}.
- **Day.** Every entry belongs to the day it was written. The list is grouped by day, newest first.

For example: at 10:30 on Sunday you write "Dentist moved to Friday". That is one entry, of the kind Personal log, on Sunday. Filter the list to Personal log and it is there; filter to another kind and it is not.

![The console on a phone: the header with Today, Voice, Chats and More; the search box; the list grouped by day; the New button at the bottom.](img/list.webp "The list on a phone. Each day has a bar with its count and a Read button.")

### Where things are

- **The header**, along the top: **LCARS**{ui} (a tap clears every filter and goes back to the top), the clock, the version, and buttons. On a phone it carries up to four buttons and **More**{ui} (⋯) for the rest. **More → Header buttons** chooses which four.
- **The list**, under the search box: every entry, grouped by day. A tap on a row opens it.
- **New**{ui}, at the bottom right on a phone: a tap starts a new entry. Hold it for more ways to start one.
- **Filters**{ui}: the **Filters**{ui} button beside the search box opens them as a drawer on a phone. On a desk they are the column on the left.

On a desk the three parts sit side by side: filters, the list, and the open entry.

![The console at a desk: the filters column on the left, the list in the middle, an entry open in the editor on the right.](img/desk.webp "At a desk. 1 and 2 fold the first two columns away.")

### A button, a key or your voice

Most things in the console can be done any of these ways:

1. **A button or a tap**, as described in each section.
2. **A key**, at a desk. Press **?** for the keyboard help: every key, searchable; Enter runs the one you pick.
3. **Your voice.** Press **Voice**{ui} (or **m**) and say "computer, …" with an order. Say "computer, help" for every phrase.

When you cannot find something, press **p** (or say "computer, command palette"). The command palette lists every filter and action by name. Type a few letters and press Enter.

This manual is in the console too: **The manual**{ui} at the foot of the help opens it in a tab of its own, and so does "computer, open the manual". On your server it is also at `/manual`: the server's address with `/manual` after it.

### What is not here

- **The deck** is the 3D ship, walked in the browser. **Deck**{ui} in the header (or **D**) opens it. It has its own controls, and this manual does not cover them, but for one: its bridge keeps the clock, at night watch (the lights low) from 23:00 to 07:00 in the server's zone (`LCARS_TZ`), and **U** there, or "night watch" and "day watch", change it until the next watch; "watch by hand" stops the clock, "watch by the clock" starts it again.
- **Setting up the server** is in the project's README on GitHub: [github.com/saftacatalinmihai/lcars](https://github.com/saftacatalinmihai/lcars).
- **A feature that is off.** Some features need something on the server, such as a key or a program. When one is off, its door says so. Press **W** for the list (see [When something goes wrong](#trouble)).

### Finding this manual again

This manual is a report in your journal. Open **Reports**{ui} (in the filters, or say "computer, reports"), and pick "The console: a user manual". In the reader, **Open as a page**{ui} shows it in a tab of its own.
