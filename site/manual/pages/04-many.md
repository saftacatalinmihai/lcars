## 4. Several entries at once {#many}

To change many entries together, tap **Select**{ui} above the list (or press **x**), pick them, then choose an action.

### Pick entries

1. Tap **Select**{ui}. Each row gets a box, and the bar shows the count on top: "nothing picked", then "3 picked".
2. Tap the rows you want. **All**{ui} picks every row the list shows; once all are picked it reads **None**{ui}, which unpicks them (or say "computer, pick none"). At a desk, Shift-click picks everything from the last pick to here, and Ctrl-click (⌘-click) picks a row without leaving the list.
3. **Done**{ui}, beside the count, leaves select mode.

The actions are **Kind**{ui}, **Schedule**{ui}, **Join**{ui} and **Delete**{ui}. On a phone they sit at the bottom of the screen, where **New** was, with **Delete**{ui} set apart in red. At a desk they are one row in the bar, **Delete**{ui} apart before **Done**{ui}.

Up to 200 entries can be changed or deleted at once. Narrow the list first with a search or a filter.

### While a change is written

One change runs at a time. A line under the count says what is being written, and the actions wait until it ends. The message at the bottom names the entries by their titles and offers **Undo**{ui}.

If a change fails on some entries, the line says so, and the picks become just the entries that did not change. Tap the same action again to retry. Offline, the line says the changes are sent when the link is back.

### Change their kind

Tap **Kind**{ui} and choose a kind. Every picked entry gets it. **Undo**{ui}, in the message, puts each one's old kind back.

### Schedule them

Tap **Schedule**{ui} to give every picked entry one day and span, or to clear their schedule. **Undo**{ui} is offered the same way.

### Join them into one

**Join**{ui} (**J**) puts the text of every picked entry into the oldest of them.

1. Pick two or more entries, and tap **Join**{ui}.
2. The console asks "Join N entries?" and shows which entry is kept and which are folded in. The kept entry keeps its kind, its title and when it was written; each folded-in text goes in under its own time.
3. Tap **Join**{ui}, or **Keep them apart**{ui}. **The newer entries are deleted when they are joined.**

They are not destroyed: **Undo**{ui}, in the message after the join, restores the others and puts the kept entry's text back. An edit made to the kept entry since the join is kept. After the message is gone, set **State**{ui} to **deleted only**{ui}, pick them, and tap **Restore**{ui}.

If some of the newer entries could not be deleted, the line says "Joined; 2 still here. Delete them to finish", with those entries picked: tap **Delete**{ui} to finish the join.

### Delete them

Tap **Delete**{ui}. The console asks "Delete N entries?"; tap **Delete N**{ui} to confirm, or **Keep them**{ui}. **Every picked entry leaves the journal.** They are not destroyed: **Undo**{ui} in the message brings them back. Later, pick them with **State**{ui} set to **deleted only**{ui}: the bar's **Delete**{ui} then reads **Restore**{ui}.
