## 2. Find and look back {#find}

To find an entry, type in **Search the journal**{ui} at the top of the list; to narrow the list, open **Filters**{ui}.

### Search

Type words in **Search the journal**{ui}. The list shows the entries holding every word, anywhere. Put "a phrase" in quotes to search it whole.

Tap **Match**{ui} for more:

- **Text**{ui}, **Regex**{ui} or **Meaning**{ui}. **Meaning**{ui} finds entries close in meaning to what you typed, closest first, even when the words differ. A `~` in front of a search does the same. Meaning needs the server's embeddings; without them, **W** says so.
- **Match case**{ui}, **Whole words**{ui}, and **Not matching**{ui} (the entries without the words).
- Where to look: **title + content**{ui}, **content only**{ui}, **title only**{ui}, or **every field**{ui}.

### Filter the list

Tap **Filters**{ui} (on a phone it opens as a drawer; tap **Done**{ui} to close it). At a desk the filters are the left column.

- **Kind**{ui}: a tap on a kind's chip turns it on or off, and the list shows the kinds that are on. Ctrl-click (⌘-click on a Mac) keeps only that one. **Find a kind**{ui} (**K**) searches every kind by name. **All kinds** (**C**) clears the kind filter.
- **State**{ui}: **live only**{ui}, **live + deleted**{ui} or **deleted only**{ui}; and **any state**{ui}, **open only**{ui} or **done only**{ui}.
- **Written**{ui}: a **since**{ui} and **until**{ui} day, or **7d**{ui}, **30d**{ui}, **month**{ui}, **clear**{ui}, and **on this day**{ui} (this date in other years).
- **Sort**{ui}: newest first by default; also by when it was last changed, by number, by length, or by kind.
- **crew entries hidden**{ui}: the entries the crew and Number One write stay out of the list until you tap it (it then says **crew entries shown**{ui}), or press **H**.

**Filters**{ui} shows a count of how many are on. Press **c** (or say "computer, clear filters") to turn them all off; the message at the bottom offers them back.

### Save a filter you use often

Set the search, the filters and the sort, then tap **+ Save this view**{ui} under **Views**{ui} (or press **V**) and give it a name. The view's chip brings it all back with one tap. Views are kept in this browser, so each device has its own.

### Go to a day or a month

- Each day in the list has a bar with its count. **Read**{ui} on the bar shows the whole day as one block.
- Drag the strip of years beside the list to scrub through the months.
- **Today**{ui}, in the header, shows today's entries by time.
- **[** and **]** move a day; **{** and **}** a month.
- **Month**{ui} (**M**) opens the calendar. See [Tasks and the calendar](#tasks).

### What is new since I looked?

Press **u**, or say "computer, what did I miss". The list shows only what arrived since your last visit.

### Look back

These are in the filters, under the activity chart:

- **Statistics**{ui}: the journal as numbers.
- **Readings**{ui} (**E**): every measured series, such as a weight, by day, week or month.
- **Resurface**{ui} (**R**): an old entry, at random.

**Graph**{ui} and **Stars**{ui} are in the header at a desk, and under **More**{ui} on a phone:

- **Graph**{ui} (**v**): every `[[page]]` the journal links, as a map.
- **Stars**{ui} (**S**): the whole journal as a starfield, placed by meaning. It needs the embeddings, as Meaning does.

Two more, by key, voice or the palette:

- **Trend**{ui} (**T**, "computer, trend a topic"): how often a name turns up, month by month.
- **Ambient mode**{ui} (**z**, "computer, ambient"): the clock, the numbers and a memory, alone on screen.

### Pin an entry to the top

**Pin**{ui}, in an open entry's ⋯ sheet, keeps it at the top of the list, on every device. Eight can be pinned at a time.
