## 3. Read and share {#read}

A tap on a row opens the entry to read; **Edit**{ui} at the top turns it into the editor, and **Read**{ui} turns it back.

![An entry open in the reader on a phone: the title, its kind, when it was written, the text drawn with its links and tasks, the Media & files bar, and Possibly related.](img/reader.webp "The reader. Links are underlined; TODO and DONE are drawn as marks.")

### Read an entry

- The reader draws the text: lists, links, quotes, code, and task marks such as **TODO** and **DONE**.
- A tap on a `[[link]]` searches the journal for that page.
- **Possibly related**{ui}, under the entry, lists entries that share its links or its rarer words.
- **‹ List**{ui} (on a phone) or Esc goes back.
- **j** and **k** open the next and the previous entry.

### Hear it read aloud

Tap the read-aloud button in the reader's top bar, or press **a**. Press it again, or say "computer, stop reading", to stop. "Pause reading" and "go on reading" hold it and carry on.

### Read several at once

At a desk, Shift-click a `[[link]]` to pin that entry beside this one. Pinned entries form a stack: **3** flips to the next, **#** unpins them all.

**Focus**{ui} (**f**) shows the open entry alone on the screen.

### Read a whole day or a thread

- **Read**{ui} on a day's bar, or **d**, shows the whole day as one block.
- An entry written with **Continue**{ui} is part of a thread. Say "computer, read the thread" for every entry of it, oldest first.

### Share an entry

1. Open the entry, then its ⋯ sheet, and tap **Share**{ui} (or press **F**).
2. In the Share sheet, tap a chip for each network you want: Hacker News, Reddit, X, Bluesky, Mastodon, LinkedIn or Threads. Each gets a card with its own text and a counter of its length, as that network counts it.
3. On a Mastodon or Bluesky card, tap **Post**{ui}: the console posts it for you (see [Post to Mastodon and Bluesky](#social)). On any card, tap **Open**{ui}: the network's own page opens in a new tab, with the text filled in. What it cannot be handed, such as a first comment, is copied for you to paste.
4. After **Open**{ui}, post it there, with the network's own button.

**The console posts only when you tap Post**, and only to Mastodon and Bluesky, once the server holds their keys. Every other network, it only fills the text in.

Back in the sheet, paste the post's link on its card and tap **Save link**{ui}. It becomes a share entry, which counts the post's numbers (see [Shares and the social side](#social)). To post later, tap **Schedule…**{ui} on a card and set a day and a time: the console makes a reminder with an alert.
