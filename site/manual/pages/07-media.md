## 7. Pictures, recordings and reports {#media}

To add a photo, a recording or a file to an entry, use the **Media & files**{ui} bar under its text: **Camera**{ui}, **Attach**{ui} or **Record**{ui}.

Files need a media store on the server. Without one, **W** lists what is missing.

### Add a picture, a video or a file

Open the entry, then tap one of the three in **Media & files**{ui}:

- **Camera**{ui} takes a photo or a video.
- **Attach**{ui} adds any file: photos, video, audio, PDFs, documents.
- **Record**{ui} records audio. It turns into **■ Stop**{ui} with the time; tap it to stop and attach the recording.

The file appears in the bar. A tap on it opens it.

On a phone, hold **New**{ui} and choose **Photo**{ui}, **Video**{ui}, **Voice memo**{ui} or **File**{ui} to start a new entry with one.

### Play a recording

Rows holding a recording or a video have a play button of their own. In an open entry, press **l** (or say "computer, play the tape") to play or pause it.

### See every file at once

**Media**{ui} in the filters (or **i**) shows every photo, video, recording and file in the journal, with the same filters as the task board.

### Read a report

A report is a page the crew or Number One published for you to read: an HTML page, a PDF, a chart or a table. Each one is an entry of the kind `report`, so it is in the journal too.

1. Tap **Reports**{ui} in the filters, or say "computer, reports".
2. Tap a report. It opens in the reader, with its pictures.
3. **Open as a page**{ui}, at the top of the reader, shows the report's own file in a tab of its own.

A report runs in a closed box: it cannot read your journal or anything else the console keeps in the browser.
