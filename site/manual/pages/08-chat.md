## 8. Chats with the computer {#chat}

To ask a question about your journal, tap **Chats**{ui} (or press **A**), then **+**, and type it in **ask about the journal**.

The computer is the chat that answers questions about your journal. It is Claude, run on your server, and it reads the journal to answer. It needs Claude on the server; without it, **W** says so.

### Ask a question

1. Tap **Chats**{ui}, then **+** for a new chat, or tap an old one in the list.
2. Type in the box at the bottom, and send.
3. The answer is written as it comes. An entry it names, such as `#123`, is a link: a tap opens it.

Or say "computer, ask what did I write about the garage", and the answer is spoken.

### The Chats list

The list is in sections. Each shows only when it has something in it:

- **Open questions**{ui}: what Number One and the crew ask of you (see [The crew](#crew)).
- **Computer**{ui}: your chats with the computer.
- **Crew**{ui}: the queue, the rooms, Number One and each engineer, if your server runs the crew.
- **Aboard**{ui}: the people you talked to on the deck, and the deck's architect.
- **Reports**{ui}: each crew role's latest report, and your chat about it. A new one is marked **NEW**{ui}. **Crew notes**{ui} is here too.
- **Courses**{ui}: the navigator of each course.
- **Archived**{ui}: chats you put away.

**search the chats**{ui}, at the top, finds a chat by its words.

### In a chat

- **⋯** at the top: **New chat**, **Read it out** (the last answer, aloud), and **Archive**{ui}.
- The model chip chooses which Claude answers the next message: Sonnet, Haiku, Opus or Fable.
- **+** beside the box adds files to your message. You can also paste one into the box, or drop it on the thread.
- To quote part of an answer, select it and press **>**. On a phone, swipe a paragraph to the right.
- A swipe on a chat in the list marks it read or unread; **Archive**{ui} puts it away. It comes back with **Bring back**{ui}.

### Talk instead of typing

Tap **Voice**{ui} at the top of a chat. Replies are then spoken as they come, and two ways to talk appear under the box:

- **Hold to talk**{ui}: hold it (or Space, at a desk) and speak; let go and it is sent.
- **A conversation**: say "computer, start conversation". The microphone stays open; each thing you say is sent when you stop, and the reply is spoken. "End conversation" stops it.

Config sets what the voice says (everything, or only the answers) and when it turns itself off (after five minutes by default).

### The chat dock, at a desk

At a desk, chats can also open as small windows at the lower right, so you can keep writing while one answers. Press **B** to go into the dock and **B** again to come back. In a window, Esc minimises it and **f** opens the full view.
