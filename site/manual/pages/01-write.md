## 1. Write an entry {#write}

To write something down, tap **New**{ui} (or press **n**), type, and leave: the console saves as you go.

### Write a new entry

1. Tap **New**{ui}. On a phone it is at the bottom right; at a desk, in the header.
2. Type a title in the top field, if you want one. An entry needs no title.
3. Tap the kind chip under the title (it says **Personal log** at first) to choose the kind. Type part of a name to narrow the list. A name nobody has used is the last row, **new kind**{ui}.
4. Write in **content**{ui}.

The entry saves 3 seconds after you stop typing, once it has words. The chip beside **content**{ui} says where it stands: **● Editing**{ui}, **Saving…**{ui}, **✓ Saved**{ui}, or **✕ Not saved · Retry**{ui}. Tap the chip, or press **Ctrl+S** (**⌘S** on a Mac), to save at once. Leaving the entry by any door (Back, Close, another row) saves it first.

### Other ways to start one

Hold **New**{ui} on a phone for its menu: **Dictate**{ui}, **Video dictation**{ui}, **Photo**{ui}, **Video**{ui}, **Voice memo**{ui}, **Task**{ui}, **File**{ui} and **From template**{ui}.

- **Quick capture**{ui} (press **q**, or say "computer, capture"): one line straight into the journal, no editor and no title. Pick the kind under it and press **Capture**{ui}. Type `/log weight 72.4` to record a number instead.
- **From a template** (press **N**): an entry that starts from one of your saved shapes. A template is an entry of the template kind (`template` unless you changed it in Config).
- **A task** (**Task**{ui} in New's menu, or "computer, new task"): an entry that starts as a `TODO` line. See [Tasks and the calendar](#tasks).

![Quick capture on a phone: one content box, the kind field, Cancel and Capture, and a chip for each kind.](img/capture.webp "Quick capture. The kind you pick is remembered for next time.")

### Write by voice

Tap **Dictate**{ui} at the bottom of an open entry. What you say is typed where the cursor is. **Video**{ui} does the same and films you; the video is attached to the entry. Say "computer, …" in the middle of dictation to give an order instead of words. See [Voice orders](#voice).

**Tidy dictation**{ui}, in the entry's ⋯ sheet, has the computer fix misheard words, spelling and punctuation. It shows the changes as a diff, and nothing changes until you accept it.

### Link, tag and shape

In **content**{ui}:

- Type `[[` to link to another page. Suggestions appear; pick one with the arrows and Enter.
- Type `#` to tag. Existing tags are suggested.
- Type `/` for the date, the time, or a shape such as a task line.
- Write `((123))` to link to entry number 123.

Lines that start with `TODO`, `DOING`, `NOW`, `LATER` or `WAITING` are tasks; `DONE` or `CANCELED` closes one.

### Change when it happened

Open **Details**{ui}, under the content. **Written**{ui} is when the entry happened; tap it to change the day or the time. **Scheduled**{ui} gives it a start and an end, which puts it on the calendar. **Done**{ui} marks it open or done. **Value**{ui} holds a number, such as a weight.

To add something you remember later, open the entry before it and press **o** (or say "computer, new entry after this one"). The new entry is stamped halfway between the two.

### Keep writing in a new entry

In the entry's ⋯ sheet:

- **Continue**{ui} (**w**) starts a new entry linked back to this one, so the two read as a thread.
- **Duplicate**{ui} (**y**) starts a new entry with a copy of this one.
- **Revert**{ui} throws away your changes and goes back to what the server holds.

### Delete an entry

1. Open the entry, then its ⋯ sheet.
2. Tap **Delete**{ui}. The console asks "Delete this entry?".
3. Tap **Delete**{ui} again to confirm, or **Keep it**{ui}.

A deleted entry leaves the list, but it is not destroyed. The message at the bottom of the screen offers **Undo**{ui} for 8 seconds. After that, set **State**{ui} in the filters to **deleted only**{ui}, open the entry, and tap **Restore**{ui}.
