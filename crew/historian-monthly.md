On the first of the month, write the chronicle of the month that has just ended.

1. Run `month` (no argument: the month just ended). It gives counts per kind and week, and every title.
2. `get <id>` the entries that look like the month's turning points; at most eight.

Report:
- Three to six bullets: what the month was about (work, family, travel, health, projects), each with ids.
- One line of numbers: entries written, the busiest week, what changed against the usual.
- One line: what was started this month and is still open, if the titles show it.
Write it as a historian: plain, specific, from the record only. Never URGENT.
