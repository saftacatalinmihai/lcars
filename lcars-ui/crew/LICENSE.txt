The crew models, textures and animation clips in this directory are derived
from three packs by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), all released under
CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain),
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ :

  - Universal Base Characters (Standard)
    https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
    the bodies, eyes, brows, skin and hair textures, and six hair styles
  - Universal Animation Library (Standard)
    https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library
    the walks, idles, sitting, talking and reaching clips
  - Universal Animation Library 2 (Standard)
    https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library-2
    Idle_FoldArms_Loop

Made here from them, and CC0 likewise:

  - hair_Short.glb, hair_Bob.glb, hair_Ponytail.glb: the pack's Simple
    Parted and Long reshaped (scripts/deck-crew-hair.py)
  - Idle_HandsBehind_Loop and Idle_PADD_Loop in clips.glb: the library's
    Idle_Loop with the arms re-posed by IK (scripts/deck-crew-poses.py)

scripts/deck-crew-build.sh fetches the three zips (deck-crew-fetch.py, into
~/.cache/lcars-deck-models, never the repo) and rebuilds all of it.
