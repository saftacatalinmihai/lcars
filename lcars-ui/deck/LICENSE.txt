The deck's models in this directory, one section per glb and its atlas.
scripts/deck-models/build.sh rebuilds them (docs/design/deck-pipeline.md).

== bridge_skylight.webp
The bridge's skylight, seen from below. Generated with Higgsfield (Seedream 5.0 Flash) on the captain's plan, 2026-10-20, the output the captain's under Higgsfield's terms; scaled to 1024² (docs/design/bridge-redesign.md, slice 6).

== bridge_{carpet,wall}_{base,norm,rough}.webp
The bridge's carpet and wall tiles. Generated with Higgsfield (Seedream 5.0 Flash) on the captain's plan, 2026-10-21, the output the captain's under Higgsfield's terms; made seamless, matched to the palette and their normal and roughness maps derived by scripts/deck-models/tiles.py (docs/design/bridge-redesign.md, 8h).
